    📜 House Rules & FAQ

    How do I earn Hound Coins?

    Posting earns 5, the Daily Howl earns 20 once a day, and other members can gift you coins.

    How do gifts work?

    Pick a gift in the Gift Den, type a member's username, and send. The coins leave your balance and land in theirs.

    How does voice/video work?

    Join a Lounge channel, allow mic/camera, and you're connected to everyone else in that channel through your browsers.
